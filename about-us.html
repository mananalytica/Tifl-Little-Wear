About us

A kidswear studio in Gulberg, still measuring by hand.

Tifl Little Wear started as a made-to-measure kidswear studio in Lahore, and grew into a small shop of ready pieces from partner brands we trust — without giving up the fitting work that got us started.

How we started

Tifl ("little one") began with a simple frustration a lot of parents in Lahore share: children's clothes that come in a size chart, not a real fit. A chest measurement that's right but a sleeve that's wrong. A kurta that fits at the shoulder but not the length.

We started taking real measurements — eleven points, by hand, the same way a tailor would for an adult — and cutting patterns to match. What began as a single tailor working out of a small space in Gulberg is now a proper studio, still doing the same fittings, still by hand.

Where we are now

Alongside made-to-measure fittings, we now carry ready-to-wear pieces from Lahore-based kidswear brands we've vetted ourselves — for families who want something today, not in five days. We also run occasional live sales, where you can buy what you see in real time.

The studio itself hasn't changed. Every fitting is still done in person or over video, every measurement still recorded by hand, and every made-to-measure piece still finished by tailors we've worked with for years.

What we care about

A few things we won't compromise on.

Real measurements

Every made-to-measure garment is cut from your child's own measurements — not a size chart, not a guess.

Hand-finished, always

Finished by tailors we've worked with for years — not a factory line, and not cut corners on stitching.

Honest about Lahore

We're a Lahore studio serving Lahore families — we don't overpromise reach or turnaround we can't deliver.
