Fabric types

Know the fabric before you pick the print.

Lawn

Light, breathable cotton weave — the everyday summer fabric for a reason. Softens with each wash.

Season: SummerCare: Machine wash

Cotton

Sturdier than lawn, holds up to daily wear and repeated washing without losing shape.

Season: All yearCare: Machine wash

Silk

Has a natural sheen and drapes well — the go-to for occasion wear that needs to photograph nicely.

Season: Any, indoorCare: Dry clean

Chiffon

Very light and slightly sheer, usually layered over a lining. Floaty movement, delicate to handle.

Season: Summer, occasionCare: Dry clean

Organza

Crisp and semi-transparent, holds structure well — common for dupattas and layered occasion pieces.

Season: OccasionCare: Dry clean

Velvet

Heavy and warm with a soft pile — built for winter, especially occasion wear that needs presence.

Season: WinterCare: Dry clean

Quality checklist

What to actually check before you buy fabric.

The same five checks our tailors use before cutting anything — tap each one off as you go.

0 / 5 checked

Hold it up to the light

A tighter, more even weave lets less light through unevenly. Patchy light spots usually mean a looser, weaker weave that won't hold up to repeated wear.

Rub a damp white cloth across it

If colour transfers onto the cloth, the dye isn't fully set — it will bleed in the wash and fade faster than it should.

Check the weight for the season

Heavier fabric (measured in GSM) suits winter and structured occasion pieces; lighter weight suits summer and everyday wear. The wrong weight for the season is the most common regret.

Ask about shrinkage

Natural fibres like cotton and lawn shrink slightly on first wash. A good tailor builds this into the cut — ask whether that's already accounted for before stitching.

Look at the finishing, not just the fabric

Even excellent fabric looks cheap with poor stitching. Check seam edges are enclosed (not fraying), embroidery threads are secure, and hems sit flat — the same standard we hold ourselves to on every piece.

✓ All five checked — you're ready to choose fabric with confidence. Bring any questions to your fitting.
