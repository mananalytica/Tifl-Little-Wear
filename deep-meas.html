Common questions

Frequently asked

How long does a made-to-measure order take in Lahore?

Usually 3 to 5 days from your fitting to delivery, once the pattern and fabric are confirmed.

Do you visit homes in Lahore for measurements?

Yes. We offer home visits anywhere in Lahore, studio visits in Gulberg III, or a self‑measured video consult.

How many measurement points do you take for a child's garment?

Eleven: chest, waist, hip, shoulder width, sleeve length, neck, back length, front length, inseam, outseam, and height.
