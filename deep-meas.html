🧵 Studio Process 📍 Gulberg III, Lahore ⏳ 3–5 days

How a single thread becomes a garment.

From the first call to the finished piece on your doorstep — six steps, each one handled by hand. No machines, no shortcuts, just the craft we've built our studio on.

📞
Step 01

Consultation call

We ask about the occasion, fabric preference and budget, and schedule a fitting — at our studio, at your home, or via video.

✂️
Step 02

The measurement

A master tailor takes 11 points — chest, waist, shoulder, sleeve, inseam, and more — recorded by hand on a fitted card.

📐
Step 03

Pattern draft

A paper pattern is cut to those exact numbers, with grow‑with‑me ease built into the seams.

🧵
Step 04

First fitting

A basted trial garment is checked on your child. Our tailor marks adjustments directly on the fabric.

🔗
Step 05

Final stitching

French seams on everyday wear, hand‑set embroidery on occasion pieces. Finished by tailors we've worked with for years.

📦
Step 06

Delivery

Pressed, folded, and delivered to your door — usually 3 to 5 days after the first fitting.

The 11 points

Every number, taken by hand.

We take the same eleven points for every child, every garment — so patterns stay consistent as they grow.

  • 01ChestAround the fullest part, under the arms
  • 02WaistNatural waistline, tape sitting flat
  • 03HipFullest part of the seat
  • 04Shoulder widthSeam to seam across the back
  • 05Sleeve lengthShoulder to wrist, arm slightly bent
  • 06NeckBase of the neck, finger's width of ease
  • 07Back lengthNape of neck to natural waist
  • 08Front lengthShoulder to hem, garment‑dependent
  • 09InseamCrotch to ankle bone, inner leg
  • 10OutseamWaist to ankle bone, outer leg
  • 11HeightFull standing height, no shoes

Common questions

Frequently asked

How long does a made-to-measure order take in Lahore?

Usually 3 to 5 days from your fitting to delivery, once the pattern and fabric are confirmed.

Do you visit homes in Lahore for measurements?

Yes. We offer home visits anywhere in Lahore, studio visits in Gulberg III, or a self‑measured video consult.

How many measurement points do you take for a child's garment?

Eleven: chest, waist, hip, shoulder width, sleeve length, neck, back length, front length, inseam, outseam, and height.
