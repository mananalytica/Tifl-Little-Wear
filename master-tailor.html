His approach

Hand-drafted patterns

No size charts — every pattern for his line starts as a fresh draft from the child's own measurements, chalked and cut by hand.

A Multan training lineage

Apprenticed under his father's shop in Multan from age 14, then twelve years cutting occasion wear before opening his own line in Lahore.

Embroidery specialist

His focus is hand-set embroidery on festive and bridal-side pieces — the lehngas and sherwanis in our shop marked "by Ustad Sattar" are his.
