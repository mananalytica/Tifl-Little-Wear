Designing this year's Eid collection: hand block prints & growth room

Craft Category 🌿 Festive Block Prints
Stitching Style ✨ Grow-With-Me Seams
Studio Master 🧵 Ameena Bibi (Master Cutter)
Published 3 July 2026

Every festival season brings the same dilemma for parents in Lahore: choosing between itchy formal wear that looks good in photos, or casual cottons that lack festive feel. This year, we decided to solve both.

1. Hand-Carved Wooden Blocks on Breathable Cotton

Instead of synthetic silks that trap heat under Lahore's afternoon sun, our Eid collection relies entirely on 100% natural long-staple cotton. Each motif is pressed by hand using traditional hand-carved wooden blocks from local artisans.

Because hand block-printing applies dye in organic layers, the fabric retains its soft breathability — giving your child festive elegance without skin irritation.

"A child should never feel restricted in their Eid clothes. If they can't run across the garden after morning prayers, the outfit has failed."

— Ustad Abdul Sattar, Chief Tailor

2. Embedded Grow-With-Me Seams

Occasion wear often gets worn twice before being outgrown. To prevent that waste, every kurta and gharara set in this collection comes with double-blind seam allowances inside sleeves and hemlines.

When your child grows next year, simply snip the primary inner stitch to unlock up to 2 inches of clean extra length — letting them wear their favorite Eid piece for three full festive seasons.
