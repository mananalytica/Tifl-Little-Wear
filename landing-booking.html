Common questions

Before you book

How long does it take?

Usually 3 to 5 days from your fitting to delivery, once the pattern and fabric are confirmed.

Do I need to come to the studio?

You can — we're in Gulberg III, Lahore — or do a self-measured video consult instead. Both take the same 11 measurement points.

Do I pay when I book?

No. Booking a fitting is free — you only pay for the garment once it's made, cash on delivery or bank transfer.
