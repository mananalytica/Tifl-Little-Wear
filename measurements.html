Our process

How we take measurements — and everything after.

Six steps, from the first call to the finished garment on your doorstep. Nothing is estimated; every number is taken by hand.

01

Consultation call

We ask about the occasion, fabric preference and budget, and schedule a fitting.

02

Studio or home visit

A tailor visits your home in Lahore, or you visit the studio — whichever your child is calmer for.

03

11-point measurement

Chest, waist, shoulder, sleeve, inseam and more, recorded by hand on a fitted card.

04

Pattern draft

A paper pattern is cut to those exact numbers, with growth ease built in.

05

First fitting

A basted trial garment is checked on your child and adjusted on the spot.

06

Stitch & delivery

Final stitching, pressing, and delivery — usually 3 to 5 days after the fitting.

The 11 measurement points

Every child, every garment — we take the same eleven points so patterns stay consistent as they grow.

  • 01ChestAround the fullest part, under the arms
  • 02WaistNatural waistline, tape sitting flat
  • 03HipFullest part of the seat
  • 04Shoulder widthSeam point to seam point, across the back
  • 05Sleeve lengthShoulder seam to wrist bone, arm slightly bent
  • 06NeckBase of the neck, finger's width of ease
  • 07Back lengthNape of neck to natural waist
  • 08Front lengthShoulder to hem, garment-dependent
  • 09InseamCrotch to ankle bone, inner leg
  • 10OutseamWaist to ankle bone, outer leg
  • 11HeightFull standing height, no shoes

Common questions

Frequently asked

How long does a made-to-measure order take in Lahore?

Usually 3 to 5 days from your fitting to delivery, once the pattern and fabric are confirmed.

Do you visit homes in Lahore for measurements?

Yes. We offer home visits anywhere in Lahore, studio visits in Gulberg III, or a self-measured video consult.

How many measurement points do you take for a child's garment?

Eleven: chest, waist, hip, shoulder width, sleeve length, neck, back length, front length, inseam, outseam, and height.
