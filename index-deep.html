How it works

11-point measurement

Every garment is drafted from your child's own measurements — not a size chart. Growth spurts are planned for, not panicked over.

Hidden growing room

We build invisible ease into side seams, hems, and waistlines. When they grow, you bring it back, we let it out — no visible stitch marks, no starting over.

Hand-finished, always

French seams on everyday wear, hand-set embroidery on occasion pieces — finished by tailors we've worked with for years, not a factory line.

Ready-to-wear from brands we trust.

Alongside our stitching line, we carry ready pieces from Lahore-based kidswear labels. Same quality, less wait.
