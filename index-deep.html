How long does a made‑to‑measure order take?

Usually 3 to 5 days from your fitting to delivery, once the pattern and fabric are confirmed.

What is "grow‑with‑me" stitching?

We build hidden ease into seams and hems. When your child grows, we let it out — no visible stitch marks, no starting over.
